⚙️ 2. Four-Stage Processing Pipeline (Member 2)

Stage 1
🛡️ CSP Gatekeeper
  • Validates hard constraints & biological invariants.
  • Prunes predator/prey pairings (e.g. Angel + Neon).
  • Enforces tank volume carrying capacity & thermal safety.
  • Filters out infeasible combinations early (fail-fast).
Stage 2
🌊 Fuzzy Inference System
  • Handles uncertainty using Mamdani Fuzzy Logic (`scikit-fuzzy`).
  • Fuzzifies multivariate telemetry & community aggression.
  • Computes continuous Ecosystem Stability Index (ESI: 0.0 to 1.0).
  • Derives linguistic health classifications (Critical/Suboptimal/Stable).
Stage 3
🧬 Population Optimizer
  • Mode-Aware Branching (Ornamental vs Breeder).
  • Ornamental Mode: Fast-path bypass (<5ms latency).
  • Breeder Mode: DEAP Multi-Objective Genetic Algorithm.
  • Balances fitness, diversity, sex ratios & fry bioload capacity.
Stage 4
💡 XAI Simulation
  • Simulates ecosystem and counterfactual parameter shifts.
  • Interactive What-If counterfactual scenario sandbox.
  • Computes stability delta (ΔESI) between states.
  • Generates deterministic natural language explanations & reasons.
